## Clause 4.4 — Registration after failing a prerequisite (corrected 2026-11-05: the starter kit said 4.2; the fetched Jun 2026 Handbook puts this rule at 4.4)

A student who has failed a prerequisite course may not register for the
course that requires it, unless the department grants written permission
in exceptional circumstances. The permission, if granted, is recorded on
the student's file and applies to that semester only.

## Clause 1.21 — Attendance requirement

A student must attend at least 80% of the lectures, tutorials and
laboratory sessions of a course. A student whose attendance falls below
80% is not permitted to sit the final examination of that course.

## Academic standing — CGPA

A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.00 to remain in good academic
standing. A student whose CGPA falls below 2.00 is placed on academic
probation for the following semester.
