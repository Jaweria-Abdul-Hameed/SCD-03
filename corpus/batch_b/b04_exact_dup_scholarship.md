# 1.1 Merit Scholarships for Undergraduate Students

FAST-NUCES awards merit scholarships each semester based on Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA). Students ranked in the top 3 percent of their graduating batch with a minimum SGPA of 3.75 and full course load (at least 15 credit hours) receive a 100 percent tuition fee waiver for the following semester. Students maintaining a CGPA of 3.50 or above qualify for the Dean's Honor List certificate.

# 1.2 Need-Based Financial Assistance (Qarz-e-Hasna)

Need-based financial aid is administered as interest-free Qarz-e-Hasna loans covering 25 percent to 100 percent of tuition fees upon verification of family income by the Financial Aid Committee. Renewal requires maintaining a minimum CGPA of 2.50 with no F grades and no disciplinary warning. Repayment begins six months after graduation in monthly installments over a maximum period of four years.
