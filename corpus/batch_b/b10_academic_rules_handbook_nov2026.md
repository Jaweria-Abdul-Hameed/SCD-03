# 1.21 Attendance Requirement (Nov 2026 Revision)

100 percent attendance is expected in all registered courses; up to 20 percent absence may be condoned for documented medical or genuine reasons. The minimum required attendance is 80 percent for undergraduate programmes and 75 percent for graduate/postgraduate programmes; falling below the threshold renders a student ineligible for the final examination. Under the November 2026 revision, students representing the university in HEC-sanctioned national competitions receive up to 5 percent additional attendance relief approved by the Dean.

# 4.4 Academic Warning and Re-Registration of Failed Courses

Students placed on academic warning must re-register failed (F) courses in the next semester when offered. A minimum CGPA of 2.00 is required for good academic standing and graduation (Clauses 5.4 and 1.25).

# 5.8 Failed or Missed Course Mandate

A failed or missed core course becomes mandatory in the subsequent regular semester if offered. Per Clause 5.10, repeating a course whose post-requisite was already passed requires a signed written undertaking; failing the repeated prerequisite turns the post-requisite grade into F.
