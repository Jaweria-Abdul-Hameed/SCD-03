# 6.1 Library Borrowing Limits and Overdue Fines

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days, while graduate students and faculty may borrow up to 8 books for 30 days. Overdue books incur a fine of PKR 20 per book per day after the due date, capped at the replacement cost of the book.
