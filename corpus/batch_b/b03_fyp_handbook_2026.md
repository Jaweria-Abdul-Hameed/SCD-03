# 3.1 FYP Eligibility and Group Composition

Undergraduate students in Computing and Engineering programs may register for Final Year Project Part I (FYP-I) only after completing at least 90 earned credit hours with a CGPA of 2.00 or higher. Each FYP group must consist of 2 to 4 students; solo projects are permitted only with written approval from the Head of Department. FYP-I and FYP-II carry 3 credit hours each (6 credit hours total) across two consecutive regular semesters.

# 3.2 Plagiarism Threshold and Defense Evaluation

Final Year Project documentation and source code undergo mandatory Turnitin similarity screening prior to final defense. The maximum permitted similarity index is 15 percent overall and less than 4 percent from any single source. Final grading weights are 40 percent from the internal supervisor, 30 percent from the internal evaluation panel, and 30 percent from the external examiner.
