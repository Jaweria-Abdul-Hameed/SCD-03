# 1.1 Undergraduate Merit Tuition Waivers

Each semester, FAST-NUCES grants merit-based scholarships determined by Semester GPA (SGPA) and Cumulative GPA (CGPA). Undergraduates ranking within the top 3 percent of their batch who achieve an SGPA of at least 3.75 while enrolled in a full load of 15 or more credit hours are awarded a 100 percent tuition waiver for the next term. Those holding a CGPA of 3.50 or higher earn placement on the Dean's Honor List.

# 1.2 Need-Based Qarz-e-Hasna Financial Support

Interest-free Qarz-e-Hasna financial assistance covers between 25 percent and 100 percent of tuition fees after family income verification by the Financial Aid Committee. To renew aid, students must hold at least a 2.50 CGPA without failing (F) grades or disciplinary warnings, and loan repayment starts six months post-graduation over up to four years in monthly installments.
