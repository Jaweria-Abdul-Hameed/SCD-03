# 2.1 Seed Grant Eligibility and Funding Cap

The Office of Research, Innovation, and Commercialization (ORIC) offers internal seed grants to full-time faculty and supervised MS/PhD researchers. The maximum funding cap per seed project is PKR 500,000 for a standard 12-month execution window. Equipment purchases above PKR 150,000 require prior procurement committee sign-off.

# 2.2 Ethics Approval and Publication Deliverables

Any research project involving human subjects, surveys, or clinical data must obtain Institutional Review Board (IRB) clearance before funds are disbursed. Grant recipients must submit at least one peer-reviewed journal or Tier-1 conference manuscript acknowledging FAST-NUCES ORIC grant support within 18 months of project initiation.
